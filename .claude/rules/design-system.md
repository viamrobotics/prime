# Viam Design System

**https://design.viam.com/guides/intro/** is the source of truth for component visuals, naming, and interaction patterns. It documents tokens (color, spacing, typography), interaction patterns, and the canonical components to mirror. Check it instead of guessing.

Look something up when:

- Adding a component or variant. Confirm the pattern exists before inventing one.
- Naming a prop, variant, or component. Reuse its vocabulary so consumers learn one name, not two.
- Describing expected visual or interaction behavior in a review. Quote it, do not approximate.

Use `WebFetch` on that URL or a deeper page under `https://design.viam.com/`. Cap at 2 or 3 lookups per session.
