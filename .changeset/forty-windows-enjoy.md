Add `@viamrobotics/prime-split-view`, resizable and rearrangeable split-pane layouts for Svelte 5. `SplitView` renders a plain binary layout tree through a `pane` snippet and writes every change through `bind:layout`, so a function binding can persist or veto it. Panes resize by dragging or with the keyboard. The `pane` snippet receives the node, and the compound parts `SplitView.DragTrigger` and `SplitView.MoveTrigger` connect a consumer's own markup to it. `DragTrigger` starts a drag from the elements inside it, immediately on a plain area and after a 250ms hold on a control, and never from a text field. A pane that is ready to drag shows an outline and carries `data-drag-armed` for styling. `MoveTrigger` enters move mode, the keyboard and click alternative to dragging, and its `child` snippet lets a menu item replace the default button. `dragIgnore` is an attachment exported from the package root that keeps a press inside a custom element from dragging. `paneLabel` names panes for assistive technology. Immutable operations edit the tree from code: `setSplitRatio`, `swapPanes`, `movePane`, `addPaneToUpperRight`, `removePane`, and `updatePaneData`.
