Add `@viamrobotics/prime-panes`, resizable and rearrangeable split-pane layouts for Svelte 5. `Panes` renders a plain layout tree of splits, panes, and stacks through a `pane` snippet and writes every change through `bind:layout`, so a function binding can persist or veto it. Panes resize by dragging or with the keyboard. The `pane` snippet receives the node and its reactive `PaneState`, and the compound parts `Panes.DragTrigger` and `Panes.MoveTrigger` connect a consumer's own markup to it. Drags run on pointer events, so mouse, pen, and touch all drag. `DragTrigger` starts a drag from the elements inside it once the pointer moves 4px on a plain area or a `MoveTrigger`, after a 250ms hold on another control, and never from a text field. A ghost follows the pointer as a tab or a pane, showing what a drop would make, and the `dragPreview` snippet replaces its content. A pane that is ready to drag shows an outline and carries `data-drag-armed` for styling. `MoveTrigger` enters move mode, the keyboard and click alternative to dragging, and its `child` snippet lets a menu item replace the default button. `dragIgnore` is an attachment exported from the package root that keeps a press inside a custom element from dragging. Several panes can share one slot as a stack. `Panes.TabList` renders a stack's tabs, and the optional `Panes.PaneBody` marks a pane's body as their tab panel. Panes stack when dropped on a `Panes.TabListDropRegion` placed inside `TabList`, or through move mode's "Stack with" placement, at a chosen position in the tab order. Tabs drag on their own without a hold and reorder within their strip, with a preview tab and animated shifting. Move mode and Alt+Shift+ArrowLeft or Alt+Shift+ArrowRight reorder them without a drag. `PaneBody`'s rect sets where a drop splits or swaps, and a drop on a center swaps whole slots. `target="stack"` on `DragTrigger` or `MoveTrigger` moves a whole stack, and `keepInactiveMounted` keeps inactive stacked panes mounted. `paneLabel` names panes for assistive technology. Immutable operations edit the tree from code: `setSplitRatio`, `swapPanes`, `movePane`, `addPaneToUpperRight`, `removePane`, `updatePaneData`, `selectPane`, `stackPanes`, `reorderPane`, `moveStack`, and `swapSlots`.
