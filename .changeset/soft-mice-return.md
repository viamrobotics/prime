Buttons compose a color with a fill: button-warning and button-info join the colors, button-outline and button-ghost are the fills, button-dark replaces button-primary, and the icon button takes the same modifiers, replacing button-outline-danger, icon-button-secondary, and icon-button-danger. The bare class is the default and the only neutral outline.
