- Requires a name: a `label`, an `aria-label`, or an `aria-labelledby`. A `Label` with no children renders the `label`.
- Generates the control's id (`controlId`) and hands it to the label's `for` and the control.
- Collects every `Hint` into the control's `aria-describedby`, and names the control from the label.
- `disabled`, `required`, and `state` set once here reach every part.
- A prop passed to a part directly always wins over the field's value.

## Anatomy

A field is a plain `<div>` around one control, with any number of labels and hints in any order. Its one part is `Field.StateIcon`, which shows the field's `state` as an icon.

```svelte
<Field label="Name">
  <Label />
  <Input /> <!-- or NumberInput, Checkbox, RadioGroup, Select -->
  <Hint />
</Field>

<Field label="Name">
  <Label />
  <Input.Group>
    <Input />
    <Field.StateIcon />
  </Input.Group>
  <Hint />
</Field>
```

## Naming the field

A field takes exactly one name, and the type rejects a field with none:

- `label` is visible text. A `Label` with no children renders it where you place it. A `Label` with children renders those instead.
- `aria-label` names the control without visible text, for a control whose purpose the layout already shows.
- `aria-labelledby` names the control by the id of a visible element elsewhere on the page, such as a heading.

The name reaches the control, never the wrapper `<div>`. A placed `Label` names the control through `aria-labelledby`. With no `Label` placed, the control takes the field's `aria-labelledby` as its own, or its `label` or `aria-label` as its `aria-label`. An `aria-label` or `aria-labelledby` passed to the control directly wins over all of them.

```svelte
<Field aria-label="Search machines">
  <Input type="search" placeholder="Search" />
</Field>
```

In development, an `Input`, `NumberInput`, `Textarea`, `FileInput`, `Checkbox`, `Switch`, `RadioGroup`, `ToggleButtons`, `Select.Trigger`, `Combobox.Input`, `DatePicker.Field`, or `DateRangePicker.Field` that renders with no accessible name logs a warning to the console.

### States

`state` is `none`, `info`, `warn`, or `error`, and `disabled` is its own flag. Both are set once on the field and reach the label, control, hint, and `Field.StateIcon`. `error` sets `aria-invalid` on the control. A disabled field reads disabled whatever its `state`.

## What the parts take from the field

| Part                        | Reads from `Field`                                                                                |
| --------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `Label`                     | `for` (the control id), `required`, `disabled`, and `label` as its text when it has no children   |
| `Hint`                      | `disabled`, `state` (error turns red, `warn` and `info` are colored too), and joins the control's `aria-describedby` |
| `Input`, `NumberInput`, `Textarea`, `FileInput` | `controlId`, `disabled`, `required`, `state`, `aria-describedby`, and the name as `aria-labelledby` or `aria-label` |
| `Checkbox`, `Switch`        | `controlId`, `disabled`, `required`, `state`, `aria-describedby`, and the name as `aria-labelledby` or `aria-label`                         |
| `RadioGroup`                | `controlId`, `disabled`, `required`, `state`, `aria-describedby`, and the name as `aria-labelledby` or `aria-label`                         |
| `ToggleButtons`             | `controlId`, `disabled`, `state`, `aria-describedby`, and the name as `aria-labelledby` or `aria-label`                                     |
| `Select` / `Select.Trigger` | `disabled` and `required` on the root, and `controlId`, `state`, `aria-describedby`, and the name as `aria-labelledby` or `aria-label` on the trigger |
| `Combobox` / `Combobox.Input` | `disabled` and `required` on the root, and `controlId`, `state`, `aria-describedby`, and the name as `aria-labelledby` or `aria-label` on the input |
| `DatePicker` / `DatePicker.Field`, `DateRangePicker` / `DateRangePicker.Field` | `disabled` and `required` on the root, and `controlId`, `state`, `aria-describedby`, and the name as `aria-labelledby` or `aria-label` on the field part |
| `Field.StateIcon`           | `state`, and renders nothing for `none`                                                           |

A `Hint` in an `error` field describes the control, but nothing announces it when it appears. To have a screen reader read an error the moment it appears, pass `role="alert"` to that `Hint`. It goes straight to the `<p>`.

## Keyboard

`Field` is a plain `<div>` and takes no keys. Each control inside keeps its own, and clicking a placed `Label` focuses the control.

## Without a field

Every part still works on its own. Wire it by hand with `for`, `id`, and `aria-describedby`, and name the control with a `Label` pointed at it or an `aria-label`:

```svelte
<Label for="email" required>Email</Label>
<Input id="email" aria-describedby="email-hint" required />
<Hint id="email-hint">We'll never share it.</Hint>
```

## Styling

`Field` renders a bare `<div>` and takes no layout stance, so pass `class` to stack or inline its parts. `controlId` is the id the field assigns to the control. Pass your own to point an external `<label for>` or a test selector at it. A plain `id` lands on the wrapper `<div>`.
