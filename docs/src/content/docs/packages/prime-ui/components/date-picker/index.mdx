- Composed from parts, so the field, its trigger, and the calendar are yours to arrange.
- One `value`, bindable. `open` is bindable too. `min` and `max` bound the selectable dates.
- `granularity` sets the smallest unit the field edits, from `day` down to `second`.
- `DatePicker.Field` takes `state` from an enclosing [`Field`](../field/), or directly. `error` and `warn` share the `input` [component class](../../tailwind-config/component-classes/) with [`Input`](../input/).
- The calendar floats in a portal, positioned to the field.
- While the calendar is open, the field shows the darker `border-gray-9` frame, as `Select.Trigger` does.

## Anatomy

Every part is a property on `DatePicker`. `Field` is the segmented input and holds the `Trigger` button that opens the calendar. `Content` is the floating panel, portaled to the body unless `portal` says otherwise, and holds the `Calendar`.

```svelte
<DatePicker>
  <DatePicker.Field>
    <DatePicker.Trigger />
  </DatePicker.Field>
  <DatePicker.Content>
    <DatePicker.Calendar />
  </DatePicker.Content>
</DatePicker>
```

## Usage

### Two-way binding

`bind:value` on the root. The value is a `DateValue` from [`@internationalized/date`](https://react-spectrum.adobe.com/internationalized/date/), or `undefined` until one is picked. `value?.toString()` prints it as ISO `YYYY-MM-DD`.

`name` submits the date as its ISO 8601 string, and submits an empty value until a date is chosen. A disabled picker submits nothing.

</Example>

### Fully controlled

Pass `bind:value` a getter and a setter. The setter can refuse a date by leaving the value as it was, and the field then shows the date the owner holds. [State & binding](../state/) explains both modes.

<Example code={controlledSource} title="Controlled.svelte">

</Example>

### In a field

An enclosing [`Field`](../field/) supplies the label, hint, `disabled`, `required`, and `state`. The visible `Label` names the segmented field.

`required` sets `aria-required` on each segment. With a `name`, the form will not submit while no date is chosen.

### Bounds

`min` and `max` take date objects from [`@internationalized/date`](https://react-spectrum.adobe.com/internationalized/date/), such as `new CalendarDate(2026, 1, 1)`. Dates outside the range cannot be selected in the calendar. A date typed outside the range marks the field `aria-invalid` and paints the same error border as `state="error"`.

### Time of day

`granularity` of `hour`, `minute`, or `second` adds a time of day to the date, with a segment for each unit down to the one named.

### Accessible names

`DatePicker.Trigger` takes `aria-label`, which defaults to "Open calendar". `DatePicker.Calendar` takes `aria-label`, which defaults to "Choose a date". It names the floating panel and starts the calendar grid's name, with the month and year appended. `DatePicker.Content` takes its own `aria-label` when the panel needs a different name. Set the trigger's and the calendar's to name them on a page that is not in English.

The previous and next month buttons take their names from the root `locale` through `Intl.RelativeTimeFormat`, such as "Last month" and "Next month" in `en-US`. They need no override.

```svelte
<DatePicker locale="de-DE">
  <DatePicker.Field>
    <DatePicker.Trigger aria-label="Kalender öffnen" />
  </DatePicker.Field>
  <DatePicker.Content>
    <DatePicker.Calendar aria-label="Datum auswählen" />
  </DatePicker.Content>
</DatePicker>
```

### Placement

`DatePicker.Content` takes `side` and `align` to place the calendar, and `sideOffset` and `alignOffset` to nudge it. It opens below the field and aligns to its end by default. The API table lists each option and its default.

### States

`disabled` disables the field and its trigger. `readonly` shows the read-only frame and disables the trigger, so the calendar cannot open and the segments cannot be edited.

## Styling

`DatePicker.Field`, `DatePicker.Trigger`, and `DatePicker.Calendar` take `class` on the element they render. `DatePicker` takes no `class`, since it renders no element of its own. `DatePicker.Trigger` takes button attributes, and its `children` replace the calendar icon. The `previousChevron` and `nextChevron` snippets on `DatePicker.Calendar` replace the chevrons in its month buttons. `DatePicker.Content` wears `z-popover`. See [z layers](../../tailwind-config/component-classes/#z-layers).

Each day in the calendar carries these data attributes, set by bits-ui:

| Attribute                     | Set when                                        |
| ----------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| `data-selected`               | The day is the chosen date.                     |
| `data-focused`                | The day holds the calendar's focus position.    |
| `data-today`                  | The day is today.                               |
| `data-outside-month`          | The day belongs to a neighboring month.         |
| `data-outside-visible-months` | The day is outside every month on show.         |
| `data-disabled`               | The day is disabled or outside `min` and `max`. |
| `data-unavailable`            | The day is unavailable.                         |

The field frame sets `data-state` to `open` or `closed`, `data-disabled`, `data-readonly`, `data-invalid` for a typed date outside `min` and `max`, and `data-field-state` for `error` and `warn`. Each segment sets `data-segment` to its part, along with `data-invalid`, `data-disabled`, and `data-readonly`.
